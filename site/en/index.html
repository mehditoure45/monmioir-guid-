What Mon Miroir does

  • One question at a time, built on your previous answer, digging where you stayed vague.
  • Guided journeys on emotions, relationships, self-confidence, decisions and work.
  • A mirror sentence at the end of each journey: a summary of what you just understood, in your own words.
  • A memory of your reflections over time, and an inner map of your patterns, emotions, relationships and decisions.

Not an AI that analyzes you. An AI that helps you understand yourself. It never starts with advice, and it makes no diagnosis: a mirror, not a guru.

Mon Miroir is a self-reflection tool, not a medical device, and it does not replace a health professional.
